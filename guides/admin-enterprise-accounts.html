Enterprise & organisation accounts

DeJAVU runs inside one regional Enterprise account (Junior Achievement Africa). After you sign in to the admin portal, pick the organisation you need for your work.

Admin portal: app.betterimpact.com/Login/Admin

1

What you see after login

Admins with access to more than one organisation land on Select an Enterprise Account. Choose the enterprise, then open the organisation account you need.

Enterprise Organisations
2

JA Africa layout (Phase 1)

Enterprise account: Junior Achievement Africa — the umbrella for DeJAVU organisations.

Main organisation (Phase 1): JA Africa Volunteer University. Use this account to create opportunities, accept new volunteer signups, and run day-to-day volunteer management.

By default, volunteers who apply via volunteers.ja-africa.org join the JA Africa Volunteer University organisation.

Other country accounts (for example DeJAVU Nigeria and DeJAVU – Rwanda) only appear if your admin login includes them. In Phase 2, more country and partner organisations will list here the same way.

3

Other ways accounts can be set up

An Enterprise account is the umbrella. Under it sit separate organisation accounts (countries, campuses, or programmes). Each has its own volunteers, opportunities, and settings.

Beyond the Phase 1 default, the platform also allows:

  • Working in a single organisation when your login is limited to that account
  • Staying at the Enterprise level to monitor all organisations (statuses, hours, schedules) when you have full enterprise admin access
  • Optional extras (shared branding, shared notes or email visibility, Regions) — usually turned on by platform support on request
  • Adding new organisation accounts later (your billing or primary enterprise admin contacts support)

Reference: Account set-up: Enterprise considerations · Optional enterprise features

Encountering issues?

  • Do not change settings under the Configuration menu unless you have been asked to.
  • For Phase 1 work, open JA Africa Volunteer University after login.
  • Contact the Digital team if you cannot access the right account.
